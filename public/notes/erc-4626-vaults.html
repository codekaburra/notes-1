ERC-4626 金庫：Shares、收益與退出

2026-10-08 · FOUNDATIONS · DEVELOPMENT · PRODUCT · RISK

先備知識：帳戶、資產與交易 · 實作流程：整合與復原

Vault 把 underlying asset 換成 share claim，再把資產放入策略。ERC-4626 統一存取與報價介面，並不保證策略安全、收益或立即提款。

三句話版本
  • 金庫把資產換成 share。存款不會改變每股價格，只有收益與損失會——你的 share 數量不變，每股值多少在變。
  • 四種操作的取整方向是固定的：你拿到的向下取整，你付出的向上取整，誤差永遠留在金庫裡。
  • totalAssets 高不代表能立刻領出。比較金庫要看淨收益、退出路徑與權限，而不是只看 APY。

誰讀哪裡：初學者 → 第一張圖的三個狀態；工程師 → 取整矩陣、存款程式碼與開發驗收；PM → 「收益與市場」那節。

Assets 不等於 shares

假設 vault 有 1,200 USDC、1,000 shares，每股約 1.2 USDC；存入 120 可得約 100 shares，未計 fee 與取整。策略賺 120 後，資產 1,440、shares 1,100，每股約 1.3091。損失也會反映在每股價格，而不一定減少你的 share 數量。

totalAssets 的定義要讀 implementation：可能包括可用 cash、已部署策略資產、收益與損失估值。totalAssets 高不代表 idle liquidity 高；帳面 claim 與立即可退出 amount 是不同數字。

四種操作的輸入與取整

操作固定輸入對使用者計算
depositassetsshares 向下
mintshares需要 assets 向上
withdrawassets需要 burn shares 向上
redeemshares拿到 assets 向下

Preview、limits 與執行

convertToAssets/convertToShares 是理想化換算；preview 方法模擬特定操作的 fees/rounding；maxDeposit/maxMint/maxWithdraw/maxRedeem 表達目前限制。Preview 不保證稍後交易成功，也不一定包含可用性限制；先讀 max，執行仍用 slippage guard。withdraw 與 redeem 是兩種指定輸入，不是任意可交換的函式。

透過合約存款：先讀上限，再對實際成交設下界——preview 是估算，不是保證

using SafeERC20 for IERC20;

// ① 先讀上限：金庫可能有 cap、暫停或白名單
require(assets <= vault.maxDeposit(receiver), "exceeds maxDeposit");

// ② preview 已含 fee 與向下取整，用它算可接受的下界
uint256 expected  = vault.previewDeposit(assets);
uint256 minShares = expected * (10_000 - slippageBps) / 10_000;

IERC20(asset).forceApprove(address(vault), assets);
uint256 shares = vault.deposit(assets, receiver);

// ③ 執行時再守一次——preview 與實際成交之間狀態可能已變
require(shares >= minShares, "slippage");
require(shares > 0, "zero shares");   // 必要但不充分：不能取代 virtual offset

Donation 如何改變股價

在天真的 vault，直接轉 underlying 會增加 assets 卻不 mint shares，下一筆 deposit 可能換到極少甚至零 shares。Virtual assets/shares 與 decimal offset 能降低首位存款攻擊；deposit minShares、mint maxAssets 與經驗證的 router 保護成交。不要只把「拒絕零 shares」當完整防禦；策略 valuation、loss socialization 與近空 vault 都需測。

收益與市場：不要只比較 APY

拆 net yield = 策略收入 + subsidy − management/performance fees − losses − operating costs，統一 denominator 與期間。補貼結束、利率下降、borrower default 都會改 net yield。比較 underlying 與 strategy exposure、提款 queue、退出 fee、oracle 與 admin，而非只看 shares 的 symbol。

PM 需回答：提款要幾筆交易、是否 pending queue、誰可 finalize、會否改 quote、損失由哪批 shares 承擔。非同步 withdrawal 的擴充不是基本 ERC-4626 的立即提款保證。

開發驗收

在 fixed fork 測 deposit/mint/withdraw/redeem、preview 與實際值、不同 decimals、零與一個 base unit、donation、fee、strategy loss、pause 與 queue。驗證不可無代價鑄造 shares、未授權不能提別人的 shares、損失不被 hide、rounding 不會累積抽走 assets。

ERC-4626 Vaults: Shares, Yield, and Exits

2026-10-08 · FOUNDATIONS · DEVELOPMENT · PRODUCT · RISK

Prerequisites: accounts, assets, and transactions · Build flow: integration and recovery

In this note

  2. Four operations and rounding
  3. Previews, limits, and execution
  4. Donation changes the exchange rate
  5. Yield economics and exits
  6. Development acceptance
  7. References

A vault exchanges underlying assets for share claims and deploys assets into strategies. ERC-4626 standardizes access and quoting; it does not guarantee strategy safety, yield, or instant withdrawals.

In three lines
  • A vault swaps assets for shares. Deposits do not move the price per share; only gains and losses do — your share count stays put while each share's value changes.
  • The four operations round in fixed directions: what you receive rounds down, what you pay rounds up, so the residue always stays in the vault.
  • A high totalAssets does not mean you can exit now. Compare vaults on net yield, exit path and permissions, not APY alone.

Where to start: beginners → the three states in the first diagram; engineers → the rounding matrix, the deposit code and development acceptance; PMs → the yield economics section.

Assets and shares are different units

A vault with 1,200 USDC and 1,000 shares has an approximate 1.2 USDC share price. Depositing 120 yields about 100 shares before fees/rounding. After the strategy earns another 120, assets are 1,440 with 1,100 shares: approximately 1.3091 USDC each. Losses can reduce share price without reducing your share count.

Read the implementation’s totalAssets definition: it may include cash, deployed strategy assets, income, and loss valuations. High totalAssets does not imply high idle liquidity. Accounting claims and immediately withdrawable amounts differ.

Four operations and rounding

MethodFixed inputUser-facing calculation
depositassetsshares round down
mintsharesrequired assets round up
withdrawassetsrequired burned shares round up
redeemsharesreceived assets round down

Previews, limits, and execution

convertToAssets/convertToShares are idealized conversions. Preview methods model operation-specific fees/rounding; maxDeposit/maxMint/maxWithdraw/maxRedeem express current limits. A preview does not promise later success and need not capture availability limits. Read max and enforce execution slippage separately. withdraw fixes assets while redeem fixes shares; their inputs are not interchangeable.

Depositing through a contract: read the limit first, then bound the actual fill — a preview is an estimate, not a guarantee

using SafeERC20 for IERC20;

// ① read the limit first: the vault may have a cap, a pause, or an allowlist
require(assets <= vault.maxDeposit(receiver), "exceeds maxDeposit");

// ② the preview already includes fees and rounding down; derive the floor from it
uint256 expected  = vault.previewDeposit(assets);
uint256 minShares = expected * (10_000 - slippageBps) / 10_000;

IERC20(asset).forceApprove(address(vault), assets);
uint256 shares = vault.deposit(assets, receiver);

// ③ guard again at execution — state may have moved since the preview
require(shares >= minShares, "slippage");
require(shares > 0, "zero shares");   // necessary but not sufficient: no substitute for a virtual offset

Donation changes the exchange rate

In a naive vault, transferring underlying directly increases assets without minting shares, so a subsequent deposit can receive very few or zero shares. Virtual assets/shares and decimal offsets reduce first-depositor attacks; enforce minShares/maxAssets through a verified integration. Rejecting zero shares alone is not comprehensive protection. Test strategy valuation, loss allocation, and nearly empty vaults.

Yield economics and exits

Separate strategy income and subsidies from fees, losses, and operating costs using a consistent denominator and time window. Ending incentives, falling rates, and defaults change net yield. Compare underlying/strategy exposure, withdrawal queues, fees, oracles, and admins rather than share symbols or APY alone.

PMs should identify transaction count, pending queues, finalization actors, quote changes, and who absorbs losses. An asynchronous withdrawal extension is not a basic ERC-4626 guarantee of instant redemption.

Development acceptance

At a fixed fork test all four methods, preview versus actual values, decimals, zero/one base-unit amounts, donations, fees, losses, pauses, and queues. Assert no free share creation, no unauthorized withdrawals, visible loss accounting, and no accumulated rounding extraction.
