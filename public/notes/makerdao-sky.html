MakerDAO 與 Sky 穩定幣

2026-09-19 · 穩定幣 · CDP · 清算拍賣 · 開發者指南

DeFi 最早、也最重要的穩定幣系統。這則講它憑什麼值一美元——不是靠銀行帳戶裡的儲備金，而是靠超額抵押、套利誘因與清算拍賣三者的互相牽制。以及它為了維持錨定，付出了什麼代價。

三句話版本
  • 先更新名稱：MakerDAO 已完成向 Sky 的品牌遷移，DAI 可升級為 USDS，MKR 以 1:24,000 轉為 SKY。兩種穩定幣目前並存，但 USDS 規模已超越 DAI。
  • 它的錨定不靠儲備金，靠誘因：價格高於 1 美元時鑄造有利可圖，低於 1 美元時還債有利可圖，套利者把價格推回去。
  • 但有個張力值得認清：為了穩住錨定而引入的 PSM（以 USDC 1:1 兌換）讓這個「去中心化穩定幣」在實務上高度依賴中心化資產。

誰讀哪裡：初學者 → 「三種穩定幣模型」與「金庫怎麼運作」；Solidity 工程師 → 清算拍賣與整合注意事項；PM／風控 → 市場背景與使用者風險，特別是 PSM 那一段。

市場背景：一場剛結束的品牌遷移

如果你讀的教學還在講「MakerDAO 與 DAI」，它多半過時了。Endgame 計畫下的改名自 2024 年 8 月啟動，整段遷移在 2026 年 8 月收尾。

對開發者的實際影響：兩種代幣、兩個地址、同一套底層系統。2026 年 4–5 月間主要交易所已強制把 DAI 餘額轉為 USDS，但鏈上兩者仍可經轉換合約互換。整合時要決定支援哪一個（或兩個都支援），並注意流動性正在往 USDS 集中。

先分清楚：三種穩定幣模型

模型憑什麼值 1 美元主要風險
法幣儲備型
（USDC、USDT）		發行方在銀行有等值美元與短債，承諾 1:1 贖回發行方信用、銀行、監管凍結、贖回門檻
超額抵押型
（USDS／DAI）		鏈上鎖定了價值更高的加密資產，任何人可驗證抵押品暴跌、清算失效、預言機、治理
演算法型
（多已失敗）		僅靠鑄造／銷毀的誘因，無實質抵押死亡螺旋——信心一失就無法回復

USDS 屬於第二類。關鍵差異在於：你不需要信任任何公司，只需要相信抵押品的價值與清算機制會運作。代價是資金效率——要造出 100 美元，得先鎖進超過 100 美元的資產。

金庫（Vault）怎麼運作

使用者開一個金庫，鎖入抵押品，鑄出穩定幣。這是一筆對自己的貸款：

錨定怎麼維持：靠套利，不靠承諾

沒有人保證 USDS 值 1 美元。維持錨定的是兩個方向都有利可圖的套利：

PSM：維持錨定的代價

套利在理論上足夠，但實務上速度不夠快。於是引入了 PSM（Peg Stability Module）：允許任何人用 USDC 1:1 無滑點換成 USDS，反向亦然。

效果立竿見影——錨定變得非常穩。但代價必須講清楚：

當大量 USDS 是由 PSM 裡的 USDC 支撐時，USDS 實際上繼承了 USDC 的中心化風險。USDC 若被凍結或脫錨，USDS 也會跟著出事。「去中心化穩定幣」這個標籤因此需要加註：它的機制是去中心化的，但它的抵押品構成可能不是。

2023 年 3 月矽谷銀行事件期間 USDC 短暫脫錨，DAI 同步被拖下水，就是這個結構性依賴的實證。這也是後來系統大幅引入現實世界資產（RWA）與多元抵押品的原因之一——但 RWA 又帶來了另一種中心化（託管方、法律管轄）。這是個尚未解決的取捨，不是已被解決的問題。

清算：荷蘭式拍賣

當金庫的抵押率跌破門檻，抵押品會被拍賣以償還債務。現行機制是荷蘭式拍賣（價格由高往低遞減）：

  1. 任何人可呼叫清算函式，啟動拍賣並獲得一筆獎勵（誘因來源）。
  2. 拍賣起始價高於市價，隨時間線性或指數下降。
  3. 買家在自己覺得划算時買入，可分批承接。
  4. 所得先償還債務與清算罰金，剩餘退還金庫持有人。

荷蘭式拍賣取代了早期的英式拍賣，關鍵理由是抗價格崩跌：2020 年 3 月的「黑色星期四」曾出現網路壅塞導致零出價得標、系統產生鉅額壞帳的事故。新機制讓買家能立即成交、並可用閃電貸即時取得資金。

開發者整合注意事項

  • 先確認是 DAI 還是 USDS：兩者是不同合約地址、不同流動性。轉換合約可雙向 1:1，但你的介面要講清楚使用者持有哪一種。
  • USDS 與 DAI 都是 18 位小數，但別假設所有穩定幣都是——USDC 是 6 位，混用會差 10¹² 倍。
  • sUSDS 是生息包裝：它的餘額或兌換率會變動，不要當成 1:1 的 USDS 快取。
  • 穩定費持續累積：讀債務時要用即時值，不要快取。
  • 治理可改參數：抵押率、穩定費、債務上限、甚至抵押品類型都由 SKY 持有者投票決定。你的假設可能被一次投票推翻。
// DAI ↔ USDS：透過官方轉換合約，1:1 雙向，無滑點
IERC20(DAI).approve(address(CONVERTER), amount);
CONVERTER.daiToUsds(msg.sender, amount);     // 反向為 usdsToDai

// 讀金庫狀態（MCD 命名很簡短：urn=金庫, ink=抵押品, art=標準化債務）
(uint256 ink, uint256 art) = VAT.urns(ilk, vaultOwner);
(, uint256 rate, uint256 spot, , ) = VAT.ilks(ilk);

// 實際債務 = art × rate（rate 內含累積的穩定費，會隨時間變大）
uint256 debt = art * rate / 1e27;            // rate 是 27 位小數（ray）

// spot 已內含抵押率折扣：ink × spot 就是「可安全借到的上限」
require(ink * spot >= art * rate, "vault unsafe");

三種精度混在一起是這裡最常見的錯誤來源：wad = 18 位、ray = 27 位、rad = 45 位。art × rate 的結果是 rad，拿去跟 wad 比較會差 10²⁷ 倍。整合前先確認每個變數的單位。

使用者風險

風險說明
抵押品集中度若大量供給由 PSM 中的 USDC 或特定 RWA 支撐，USDS 就繼承了那些資產的風險。這是最該持續留意的一項。
清算罰金被清算不只失去抵押品，還要付罰金。行情劇烈時，維持高抵押率比事後補救便宜得多。
預言機延遲系統按預言機價格判定清算。在暴跌中預言機更新的時點可能讓你在「看起來還安全」時就被清算。
治理風險SKY 持有者可調整所有關鍵參數。治理權集中或被攻擊時，規則可能對你不利地改變。
壞帳的最終承擔者若拍賣所得不足以覆蓋債務，缺口由系統盈餘吸收；不足時會增發治理代幣稀釋持有者。
儲蓄利率不是無風險SSR 的收益來自穩定費與 RWA 收益，由治理設定，可隨時調整，也承擔上述所有系統性風險。

MakerDAO, Sky, and the Stablecoin

2026-09-19 · STABLECOINS · CDP · LIQUIDATION AUCTIONS · DEVELOPER GUIDE

DeFi's oldest and most consequential stablecoin system. This note is about what actually makes it worth a dollar — not reserves in a bank account, but over-collateralisation, arbitrage incentives and liquidation auctions holding each other in check. And what it has cost to keep the peg.

In three lines
  • Names first: MakerDAO has completed its migration to Sky; DAI upgrades to USDS, and MKR converts to SKY at 1:24,000. Both stablecoins circulate, but USDS has overtaken DAI in size.
  • The peg rests on incentives, not reserves: above $1 it pays to mint and sell, below $1 it pays to buy back and repay, and arbitrageurs push the price home.
  • One tension worth naming: the PSM that stabilises the peg (1:1 swaps against USDC) makes this "decentralised stablecoin" heavily dependent on centralised assets in practice.

Where to start: beginners → "Three stablecoin models" and "How a vault works"; Solidity engineers → liquidation auctions and integration notes; PMs and risk → market background and user risk, especially the PSM section.

Market background: a migration that just ended

If the tutorial you are reading still says "MakerDAO and DAI", it is probably out of date. The Endgame rebrand began in August 2024 and the migration wrapped up in August 2026.

What this means for developers: two tokens, two addresses, one underlying system. Major exchanges force-converted DAI balances to USDS during April–May 2026, but on-chain both remain swappable through the converter. Decide which you support (or both), and note that liquidity is consolidating into USDS.

First, three stablecoin models

(USDC, USDT)		The issuer holds dollars and short bills, promising 1:1 redemptionIssuer credit, banking, regulatory freezes, redemption gates

USDS is the second kind. The key difference is that you need trust no company, only that the collateral holds value and liquidation works. The cost is capital efficiency — minting $100 requires locking more than $100.

Nobody guarantees USDS is worth a dollar. The peg is held by arbitrage that pays in both directions:

The PSM, and what the peg costs

Arbitrage is sufficient in theory but too slow in practice. Hence the Peg Stability Module: anyone may swap USDC for USDS 1:1 with no slippage, and back again.

The effect is immediate — the peg becomes very tight. But the cost should be stated plainly:

When a large share of USDS is backed by USDC sitting in the PSM, USDS effectively inherits USDC's centralisation risk. If USDC is frozen or depegs, USDS follows. The label "decentralised stablecoin" therefore needs a footnote: the mechanism is decentralised, but the collateral mix may not be.

When USDC briefly depegged during the Silicon Valley Bank episode in March 2023, DAI was dragged along with it — direct evidence of that structural dependency. It is also part of why the system later moved heavily into real-world assets and a broader collateral mix — but RWAs introduce a different centralisation (custodians, legal jurisdiction). This is an unresolved trade-off, not a solved problem.

Liquidation: a Dutch auction

When a vault falls below its collateral ratio, the collateral is auctioned to repay the debt. The current mechanism is a Dutch auction, with the price descending from a high start:

  1. Anyone may call the liquidation function, starting the auction and earning a reward (the incentive).
  2. The starting price sits above market and decreases over time, linearly or exponentially.
  3. Buyers take as much as they like, whenever the price looks good, in partial fills.
  4. Proceeds first cover the debt and the liquidation penalty; anything left returns to the vault owner.

Dutch auctions replaced the earlier English format mainly for resilience to crashes: during "Black Thursday" in March 2020, network congestion allowed zero-bid wins and left the system with substantial bad debt. The new design lets buyers fill instantly and fund themselves with a flash loan.

Integration notes

  • Confirm DAI versus USDS: different contracts, different liquidity. The converter is 1:1 both ways, but your interface should be explicit about which the user holds.
  • USDS and DAI both use 18 decimals — but do not assume all stablecoins do; USDC uses 6, and mixing them is a factor of 10¹².
  • sUSDS is a yield wrapper: its balance or exchange rate moves, so never cache it as 1:1 USDS.
  • Stability fees accrue continuously: read debt live, never from a cache.
  • Governance can change parameters: collateral ratios, stability fees, debt ceilings, even which collateral is accepted, are all SKY votes. Your assumptions can be overturned by one proposal.
// DAI to USDS: via the official converter, 1:1 both ways, no slippage
IERC20(DAI).approve(address(CONVERTER), amount);
CONVERTER.daiToUsds(msg.sender, amount);     // usdsToDai goes the other way

// Read a vault (MCD names are terse: urn=vault, ink=collateral, art=normalised debt)
(uint256 ink, uint256 art) = VAT.urns(ilk, vaultOwner);
(, uint256 rate, uint256 spot, , ) = VAT.ilks(ilk);

// Actual debt = art x rate (rate carries accrued stability fees and grows over time)
uint256 debt = art * rate / 1e27;            // rate has 27 decimals (a "ray")

// spot already includes the collateral-ratio haircut: ink x spot is the safe ceiling
require(ink * spot >= art * rate, "vault unsafe");

Mixing the three precisions is the classic bug here: wad = 18 decimals, ray = 27, rad = 45. The product art * rate is a rad, and comparing it to a wad is off by 10²⁷. Confirm the unit of every variable before integrating.

User risk

RiskWhat it is
Collateral concentrationIf much of the supply is backed by PSM USDC or particular RWAs, USDS inherits those risks. This is the one to keep watching.
Liquidation penaltyBeing liquidated costs more than the collateral sold — there is a penalty on top. In volatile markets, keeping a high ratio is far cheaper than reacting late.
Oracle lagLiquidation follows the oracle, not the screen. In a sharp fall, the update timing can liquidate you while the market "looks" still safe.
Governance riskSKY holders can adjust every key parameter. Concentrated or captured governance can change the rules against you.
Who absorbs bad debtIf auction proceeds fall short, the surplus buffer absorbs it; beyond that, governance tokens are minted, diluting holders.
The savings rate is not risk-freeSSR yield comes from stability fees and RWA income, is set by governance, can change at any time, and carries all of the systemic risks above.
