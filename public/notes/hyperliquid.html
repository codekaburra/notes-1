Hyperliquid 架構

2026-09-09 · 永續合約 DEX · 內容截至 2026-01，變動快，落地前請再查證

Hyperliquid 並非一般的 DEX，而是一條為永續合約而生的 L1 區塊鏈。理解它，關鍵在於幾項與其他鏈上永續交易所的根本差異。

鏈上中央限價訂單簿（CLOB）

多數鏈上永續 DEX 或以預言機定價（如 GMX），或將訂單簿置於鏈下（如早期的 dYdX）。Hyperliquid 將整個訂單簿放上鏈，並以自有的共識機制（HyperBFT）支撐低延遲撮合。這是它最核心的取捨：追求接近中心化交易所的交易體驗，同時維持鏈上的透明與可驗證。

兩層架構

層職責
HyperCore原生層，負責訂單簿、撮合、清算與永續合約邏輯
HyperEVM相容 EVM 的通用合約層，可部署一般智慧合約

兩者共享狀態，因此 HyperEVM 上的合約可以直接與 HyperCore 的訂單簿互動——這是一般「EVM 鏈 + 外掛交易所」架構做不到的整合程度。

HLP 與代幣

  • HLP：社群可存入資金的做市與清算金庫，賺取價差與清算收益，等同於讓散戶成為造市者與清算對手方的資金池。
  • HYPE：原生代幣。

建議的學習路徑

  1. 先理解「為何鏈上訂單簿難以實現，Hyperliquid 如何解決」。
  2. 再看 HyperCore 與 HyperEVM 的分層與共享狀態。
  3. 最後研究 HLP 與 vault 的經濟設計。

Hyperliquid Architecture

2026-09-09 · Perp DEX · As of 2026-01; moves fast, re-verify before building

Hyperliquid is not an ordinary DEX but an L1 blockchain purpose-built for perpetual futures. To understand it, focus on a few fundamental differences from other on-chain perp exchanges.

On-chain central limit order book (CLOB)

Most on-chain perp DEXs either price via an oracle (e.g. GMX) or keep the order book off-chain (e.g. early dYdX). Hyperliquid puts the entire order book on-chain and uses its own consensus (HyperBFT) to sustain low-latency matching. This is its core trade-off: aim for a near-CEX trading experience while keeping on-chain transparency and verifiability.

Two-layer architecture

LayerResponsibility
HyperCoreThe native layer: order book, matching, liquidations, perp logic
HyperEVMAn EVM-compatible general contract layer for ordinary smart contracts

The two share state, so contracts on HyperEVM can interact directly with HyperCore's order book — an integration the usual "EVM chain plus bolted-on exchange" architecture cannot match.

HLP and the token

  • HLP: a market-making and liquidation vault the community can deposit into, earning spread and liquidation revenue — a pool that lets retail act as market maker and liquidation counterparty.
  • HYPE: the native token.

Suggested learning path

  1. First understand why an on-chain order book is hard, and how Hyperliquid solves it.
  2. Then study the HyperCore / HyperEVM split and their shared state.
  3. Finally, examine the economics of HLP and the vaults.
