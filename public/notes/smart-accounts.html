Smart Account 全貌

2026-09-09 · 以太坊 / 帳戶抽象

ERC-4337 說明的是「如何讓智慧帳戶送出交易」，而智慧帳戶本身是更廣的概念：以一個智慧合約作為使用者的錢包與帳戶，取代 EOA。合約持有資產，合約之內寫明「誰有權動用、依何種規則動用」。

四項基本能力

能力作用對應介面
保管資產存放於合約，而非私鑰之下—
授權邏輯驗證方式可任意定義：多簽、passkey、session key、每日上限validateUserOp（4337）
執行代帳戶對外呼叫合約，且可一次執行多筆execute() / executeBatch()
簽章驗證使 dApp 能驗證此合約帳戶的簽名isValidSignature（ERC-1271）

其中 ERC-1271 常被忽略卻十分關鍵。EOA 的簽章任何人皆可驗證，但合約沒有私鑰，因此須由 dApp 呼叫合約的 isValidSignature，詢問「此簽名是否有效」。缺少此介面，智慧帳戶便無法登入 OpenSea、簽署 permit 等離線簽名情境。

模組化：ERC-7579 與 ERC-6900

模組化是 2024 至 2025 年的主線。與其將所有邏輯寫死於帳戶之內，不如做成可插拔的模組：

  • validator：定義驗簽方式
  • executor：定義執行策略
  • hook：於執行前後攔截

兩套標準相互競爭：ERC-7579（較輕量，目前較被看好）與 ERC-6900。

常見實作

實務上不會自行撰寫，而是選用經過審計的成熟方案：Safe（老牌多簽）、Kernel（ZeroDev）、Coinbase Smart Wallet、Alchemy Modular Account、Biconomy Nexus。

三層關係

smart account 是合約錢包本身 → ERC-4337 讓它能被代送交易 → ERC-7579 / 6900 讓它的功能可如插件般拼裝。

延伸閱讀：本站〈帳戶抽象：ERC-4337 與 EIP-7702〉一則，說明智慧帳戶如何實際送出交易，以及 EIP-7702 如何讓既有的 EOA 原地升級為智慧帳戶。

What a Smart Account Is

2026-09-09 · Ethereum / Account Abstraction

ERC-4337 describes how a smart account gets its transactions sent; the smart account itself is the broader idea: using a smart contract as the user's wallet and account, in place of an EOA. The contract holds the assets, and inside it is written who may move them and under what rules.

Four basic capabilities

CapabilityWhat it doesInterface
CustodyAssets live in the contract, not under a private key—
AuthorisationAny validation logic: multisig, passkey, session keys, daily capsvalidateUserOp (4337)
ExecutionCalls other contracts on the account's behalf, several at onceexecute() / executeBatch()
Signature checkLets a dApp verify this contract account's signatureisValidSignature (ERC-1271)

ERC-1271 is often overlooked yet essential. Anyone can verify an EOA's signature, but a contract has no private key, so a dApp must call the contract's isValidSignature to ask "is this signature valid?". Without it, a smart account cannot sign into OpenSea, sign a permit, or handle other off-chain signing flows.

Modularity: ERC-7579 and ERC-6900

Modularity was the main theme of 2024–2025. Rather than hardcoding all logic into the account, make it pluggable:

  • validator: defines how signatures are checked
  • executor: defines execution strategy
  • hook: intercepts before and after execution

Two standards compete: ERC-7579 (lighter, currently favoured) and ERC-6900.

Common implementations

In practice you do not write your own; you pick an audited, mature one: Safe (the veteran multisig), Kernel (ZeroDev), Coinbase Smart Wallet, Alchemy Modular Account, Biconomy Nexus.

Three layers

A smart account is the contract wallet itself → ERC-4337 gets its transactions relayed → ERC-7579 / 6900 let its features be assembled like plugins.

See also the note "Account Abstraction: ERC-4337 & EIP-7702" on this site, on how a smart account actually sends transactions and how EIP-7702 upgrades an existing EOA into one in place.
