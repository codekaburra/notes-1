Aave 借貸協議

2026-09-19 · DEFI · 借貸 · 清算 · 開發者指南

DeFi 三大支柱之一。這則拆開它的四個核心：aToken 怎麼計息、利率怎麼決定、健康度怎麼算、清算怎麼發生——以及整合時最容易算錯的地方。

三句話版本
  • Aave 是超額抵押的資金池借貸：你存進去的每種資產都能當抵押品，借出的價值必須始終低於抵押品價值乘以其 LTV。
  • 利率不是人訂的，而是由資金利用率自動決定——一條在「最適利用率」處急轉向上的折線，目的是把資金池推回可提領的狀態。
  • 健康度低於 1 就會被清算，而且因為閃電貸讓清算競爭極度高效，破線幾乎等於立刻被平倉，沒有寬限期。

誰讀哪裡：初學者 → 「存與借的代幣流」與「健康度」；Solidity 工程師 → 整合程式碼與清算機制；PM／風控 → 市場背景與使用者投資風險。

存與借：代幣流

Aave 的帳目全部用代幣表示，這讓部位可組合、也可轉移：

利率：由利用率決定

利用率 U = 已借出 / 總存入。利率隨 U 上升，並在最適利用率（U_optimal）處急轉直上——這個轉折是刻意的，目的是在資金快被借光時用高利率逼借款人還錢、同時吸引新存款，好讓存款人始終能提領。

健康度與清算

Aave 用健康度（Health Factor）一個數字總結你的部位安全程度：

Health Factor = Σ(抵押品價值 × 清算門檻) / Σ(借款價值)

// HF > 1  安全；HF = 1 臨界；HF < 1 可被任何人清算

注意分子用的是清算門檻（Liquidation Threshold），不是 LTV。兩者不同且都重要：

參數意義
LTV你最多能借到多少。例如 ETH 的 LTV 80%，存 $100 最多借 $80。
Liquidation Threshold你撐到哪裡會被清算。通常比 LTV 高幾個百分點（如 82.5%），中間那段就是你的緩衝。
Liquidation Bonus清算人的獎勵（如 5%）。這筆錢從你的抵押品扣——這就是被清算的實際代價。

整合程式碼

// 存入（先 approve 給 Pool）
IERC20(asset).approve(address(POOL), amount);
POOL.supply(asset, amount, onBehalfOf, 0);

// 借出：interestRateMode 2 = variable
POOL.borrow(asset, amount, 2, 0, onBehalfOf);

// 還款：amount 傳 type(uint256).max 表示「全部還清」
IERC20(asset).approve(address(POOL), type(uint256).max);
POOL.repay(asset, type(uint256).max, 2, onBehalfOf);

// 提領：同樣可用 max 表示全部
POOL.withdraw(asset, type(uint256).max, to);
// 讀部位狀態——上線前務必監控 healthFactor
(
    uint256 totalCollateralBase,
    uint256 totalDebtBase,
    uint256 availableBorrowsBase,
    uint256 currentLiquidationThreshold,
    uint256 ltv,
    uint256 healthFactor        // 1e18 = 1.0
) = POOL.getUserAccountData(user);

require(healthFactor > 1.05e18, "too close to liquidation");

兩個容易踩的坑：Base 單位不是該資產的單位——getUserAccountData 回傳的是以基準貨幣（通常 8 位小數的 USD）計價的總額，不要跟代幣數量混用。以及healthFactor 在沒有借款時會回傳 type(uint256).max，直接比大小的判斷要先處理這個情況。

V3 的幾個關鍵設定

  • E-Mode（效率模式）：當抵押品與借款資產高度相關（如 ETH 與 wstETH、各種穩定幣之間），可切換到更高的 LTV。代價是該模式下只能借特定類別的資產。
  • Isolation Mode（隔離模式）：風險較高的新資產只能作為唯一抵押品，且有鑄造上限、只能借白名單穩定幣。目的是把壞帳侷限在該資產。
  • Siloed Borrowing（隔離借款）：某些資產一旦借了，就不能同時借其他資產。
  • Supply / Borrow Caps：每種資產的總量上限，防止單一資產的風險無上限累積。整合時要處理「達到上限而交易失敗」的情況。

智慧合約安全風險（開發者）

  • 不要快取 aToken 或債務餘額：它們每個區塊都在變，快取值會讓你的會計慢慢偏離真實。
  • 健康度要留緩衝：以 1.0 為目標等於立刻被清算。自動化策略應以 1.3–1.5 以上為操作區間，並考慮預言機更新的跳動。
  • 處理上限與暫停：supply/borrow cap 達標、資產被凍結或下架，都會讓交易 revert。整合要有降級路徑。
  • 預言機依賴：Aave 用外部預言機定價。若你的策略疊在 Aave 之上，你同時繼承了它的預言機風險。
  • E-Mode 切換會改變健康度：進出 E-Mode 會即時改變 LTV 與清算門檻，自動化流程要重算。

使用者投資風險

風險說明
清算沒有寬限期機器人以毫秒競爭，HF 破 1 通常在同一個區塊內就被清算，並從抵押品扣走清算獎勵。
利率會瞬間跳升利用率越過轉折點後，借款利率可能在數小時內從個位數跳到數十個百分點。浮動利率借款要持續盯。
存款未必隨時可提當利用率接近 100%，池中沒有閒置資金，提領會失敗——只能等還款或加息吸引新存款。
壞帳由存款人承擔若價格瞬間崩跌導致清算不及，抵押品不足以覆蓋債務，缺口最終由該資產的存款人分攤。
預言機風險清算依預言機價格而非你看到的市場價。預言機異常或延遲可能觸發非預期清算。
循環槓桿會放大一切存款→借款→再存入的循環操作能把曝險放到數倍，同時把清算距離壓到極薄。

Aave: Lending Mechanics

2026-09-19 · DEFI · LENDING · LIQUIDATION · DEVELOPER GUIDE

One of DeFi's three pillars. This note opens up its four core pieces — how aTokens accrue, how rates are set, how health is computed, how liquidation happens — and where integrations most often get the maths wrong.

In three lines
  • Aave is over-collateralised pooled lending: everything you supply can serve as collateral, and borrowed value must stay below collateral value times its LTV.
  • Rates are not set by anyone; they are a function of utilisation — a kinked curve that turns sharply upward at an "optimal" point, designed to push the pool back to a withdrawable state.
  • Below a health factor of 1 you are liquidated, and because flash loans make liquidation fiercely competitive, crossing the line means near-instant closure with no grace period.

Where to start: beginners → "Token flows" and "Health factor"; Solidity engineers → the integration code and liquidation mechanics; PMs and risk → market background and user investment risk.

V4 changes the architecture (modular hubs and spokes, configurable risk), but V3's core concepts — aTokens, utilisation-driven rates, health factor, liquidation — carry over, so learning V3 is not wasted.

Supplying and borrowing: token flows

Aave represents every position as a token, which makes positions composable and transferable:

Rates: a function of utilisation

Utilisation is U = borrowed / supplied. Rates rise with U and turn sharply upward at the optimal utilisation point. That kink is deliberate: when funds are nearly exhausted, a punishing rate pushes borrowers to repay and attracts new deposits, so suppliers can still withdraw.

Health factor and liquidation

Aave compresses position safety into one number, the health factor:

Health Factor = Σ(collateral value × liquidation threshold) / Σ(borrow value)

// HF > 1  safe;  HF = 1 at the edge;  HF < 1 anyone may liquidate you

Note the numerator uses the liquidation threshold, not LTV. They differ, and both matter:

ParameterMeaning
LTVHow much you may borrow. With ETH at 80% LTV, $100 supplied allows $80 borrowed.
Liquidation thresholdHow far you can drift before liquidation. Usually a few points above LTV (say 82.5%); the gap is your buffer.
Liquidation bonusThe liquidator's reward (say 5%), taken out of your collateral — this is what being liquidated actually costs.

Integration code

// Supply (approve the Pool first)
IERC20(asset).approve(address(POOL), amount);
POOL.supply(asset, amount, onBehalfOf, 0);

// Borrow: interestRateMode 2 = variable
POOL.borrow(asset, amount, 2, 0, onBehalfOf);

// Repay: passing type(uint256).max means "repay everything"
IERC20(asset).approve(address(POOL), type(uint256).max);
POOL.repay(asset, type(uint256).max, 2, onBehalfOf);

// Withdraw: max works here too
POOL.withdraw(asset, type(uint256).max, to);
// Read position state — always monitor healthFactor before going live
(
    uint256 totalCollateralBase,
    uint256 totalDebtBase,
    uint256 availableBorrowsBase,
    uint256 currentLiquidationThreshold,
    uint256 ltv,
    uint256 healthFactor        // 1e18 = 1.0
) = POOL.getUserAccountData(user);

require(healthFactor > 1.05e18, "too close to liquidation");

Two easy traps: "Base" units are not the asset's units — getUserAccountData returns totals in the base currency (usually USD with 8 decimals), so never mix them with token amounts. And healthFactor returns type(uint256).max when there is no debt, so a naive comparison needs that case handled first.

Key V3 configurations

  • E-Mode: when collateral and borrowed asset are highly correlated (ETH and wstETH, or stablecoins among themselves), you can switch to a much higher LTV. The trade-off is that you may only borrow assets in that category.
  • Isolation mode: a riskier new asset may only be used as the sole collateral, with a debt ceiling and a stablecoin allowlist. The point is to confine bad debt to that asset.
  • Siloed borrowing: some assets, once borrowed, prevent borrowing anything else simultaneously.
  • Supply / borrow caps: per-asset ceilings so exposure cannot accumulate without limit. Integrations must handle the "cap reached, transaction reverts" case.

Smart-contract security (developers)

  • Never cache aToken or debt balances: they change every block, and a cached value drifts your accounting away from reality.
  • Keep health-factor headroom: targeting 1.0 means instant liquidation. Automated strategies should operate at 1.3–1.5 or above, allowing for oracle jumps.
  • Handle caps and pauses: hitting a supply/borrow cap, or an asset being frozen or offboarded, reverts transactions. Have a degraded path.
  • Oracle dependency: Aave prices via external oracles. A strategy built on Aave inherits Aave's oracle risk.
  • Toggling E-Mode changes health: entering or leaving E-Mode instantly changes LTV and threshold; recompute in automated flows.

User investment risk

RiskWhat it is
No grace period on liquidationBots compete in milliseconds; crossing HF 1 usually means liquidation in the same block, with the bonus taken from your collateral.
Rates can spike instantlyPast the kink, borrow rates can go from single digits to tens of percent within hours. Variable-rate borrowing needs watching.
Deposits are not always withdrawableNear 100% utilisation there is no idle liquidity and withdrawals fail — you wait for repayments or for higher rates to attract deposits.
Bad debt falls on suppliersIf a price gaps down faster than liquidation can clear, collateral may not cover the debt, and the shortfall is ultimately borne by that asset's suppliers.
Oracle riskLiquidation uses the oracle price, not the market price you see. An anomaly or lag can trigger unexpected liquidation.
Looped leverage magnifies everythingSupply → borrow → re-supply loops multiply exposure while compressing the distance to liquidation to almost nothing.
